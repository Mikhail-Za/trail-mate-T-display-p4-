Broadleaf Plantain (Plantago major)

== WHERE ==
- Compacted, trampled soil: footpaths, driveways, lawns, trailheads, campsite edges across nearly all of North America.
- Leaves spring through fall; seed spikes summer into fall.

== IDENTIFY ==
- Growth: a low, ground-hugging basal rosette in open, walked-on ground.
- Leaves: broad ovals with 3 to 7 strong parallel veins running the full length of the leaf.
- String test: tear a leaf slowly across; the veins pull out as tough, stringy threads. This is the signature.
- Flower stalk: leafless, upright, topped with a long slender spike of tiny green flowers that becomes a rat-tail of seeds.
- Sap: none of it milky; leaf stalks often purplish at the base.

== LOOKALIKES ==
- Lily of the valley: DEADLY, and it also has smooth parallel-veined leaves. Differences: it grows in shaded woods and old gardens from spreading underground runners, its two or three leaves stand upright wrapping a single central stalk (never a flat rosette in trampled ground), it hangs small white bells that become red berries, and its veins do not pull out as strings. If you are in shade near plantings and there is no rat-tail seed spike, do not pick.
- False hellebore (Veratrum): DEADLY. A tall plant of wet meadows with large, heavily pleated, accordion-ribbed leaves up a stout stalk. Nothing like a flat rosette, but it fools people hunting "big parallel-veined leaves."
- The safe combination to demand: flat rosette + trampled open ground + stringy veins + rat-tail spike.

== EDIBLE PARTS AND PREPARATION ==
- Young leaves: raw in salads when small and tender.
- Older leaves: stringy and tougher; boil or steam, and strip out the biggest veins.
- Seeds: strip the spikes when brown; eat raw or cooked, or dry and grind. They are a psyllium relative and rich in fiber.

== SEASON ==
- Leaves: spring through fall; smallest and most tender in spring before they toughen and string up.
- Seeds: late summer into fall, once the rat-tail spikes turn brown and dry.

== CAUTION ==
- No serious toxicity concerns at food quantities.
- The fiber-rich seeds absorb water; eat them with plenty of fluid.
- Confirm the rosette-plus-rat-tail combination every time before picking broad shiny leaves anywhere near gardens or shade.
