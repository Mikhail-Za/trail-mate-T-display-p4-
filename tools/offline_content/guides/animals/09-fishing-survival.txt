Fishing for Survival

== LEGAL AND ETHICAL FIRST ==
- Fishing is regulated everywhere: licenses, seasons, limits, and legal methods vary by state and by water. In normal times, follow those rules and carry a license.
- The methods below beyond a simple hook and line (traps, spears, multiple set lines) are illegal for recreation in most places. They belong to a genuine survival emergency only. Know your local regulations.
- Take only what you will eat. Even in a survival situation, waste helps no one.

== WHERE FISH HOLD ==
- Fish rest where they are sheltered and fed: undercut banks, submerged logs and roots, boulders, weed edges, and the deep outside of river bends.
- In current, fish face upstream and wait behind rocks and in the slow seam beside fast water. Fish the edge between fast and slow.
- In heat, fish go deep or into shade and feed at dawn and dusk. In cool weather they may feed in shallow sun. The inlets and outlets of ponds concentrate fish.

== SIMPLE METHODS ==
- Hook and line is the most reliable. Improvise hooks from wire, thorns, bone, or a can pop-tab, and line from cordage inner strands. Bait with worms, grubs, insects, or a scrap of bright cloth or shiny metal jigged in the water.
- A set line, also called a bank line: tie a baited hook to a springy branch or an anchored line and let it fish while you do other tasks. Check it often.
- Fish trap: a funnel of stones or sticks in a stream that lets fish in and not out, or a bottle-style minnow trap. Works best in narrow flowing water.
- Spearing works only in clear shallow water. Remember that light bends at the surface, so aim low, below where the fish appears to be.

== HANDLING YOUR CATCH ==
- Kill fish quickly with a sharp knock to the head; do not let them suffocate slowly.
- Bleed and gut them soon and keep the meat cool. Fish spoil fast.

== SEASON ==
- Many fish spawn in spring, moving into shallow streams and lake edges where they gather but are often legally protected. In survival only, that concentration is food; in normal times, leave spawning fish alone.
- Cold-water fish such as trout stay active in cool weather; warm-water fish such as bass, panfish, and catfish feed best in warm months.

== CAUTION: EATING FISH ==
- Freshwater fish can carry parasites and flukes. Cook fish thoroughly until it is opaque and flakes apart. Do not eat raw survival-caught fish.
- Avoid fish that look diseased (sores, foul smell, mushy flesh) or that come from water you would not drink (stagnant, polluted, or algae-choked).
- Full cleaning and cooking guidance is in the preparing wild game and fish article.
