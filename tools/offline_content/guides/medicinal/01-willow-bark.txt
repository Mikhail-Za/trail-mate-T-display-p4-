Willow Bark (Salix species): pain and fever

== WHAT IT DOES ==
- The inner bark of willow contains salicin, the natural relative of aspirin.
- The body turns salicin into salicylic acid, which can ease pain, fever, and inflammation.
- It is a weak, slow aspirin, not a strong painkiller. Expect mild relief at best.

== CRITICAL WARNINGS: READ BEFORE USE ==
- This IS the aspirin family. Every aspirin warning applies to it.
- Do NOT use if you are allergic to aspirin or to anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs).
- Do NOT use if you are on blood thinners, or already taking aspirin or ibuprofen. Bleeding risk stacks.
- NEVER give it to a child or teenager who has a fever or viral illness. It risks Reye's syndrome, a rare but deadly brain and liver reaction. This rule is absolute.
- Avoid it with stomach ulcers or any history of stomach bleeding. Salicylates irritate the gut.
- Avoid it in pregnancy and while nursing.
- Stop immediately if you get ringing in the ears, nausea, or stomach pain. Those are early signs of too much salicylate.

== WHERE ==
- Willows grow along streams, rivers, ponds, and other wet ground across nearly all of North America.

== IDENTIFY ==
- Shrubs or small trees in wet ground, with slender, flexible twigs that bend without snapping.
- Leaves: narrow, long, lance-shaped, often finely toothed, usually pale or silvery underneath, set singly (alternate) along the twig.
- Catkins: the fuzzy "pussy willow" flower spikes in spring.
- Fresh inner bark and twigs taste bitter.

== LOOKALIKES ==
- Willow has no deadly lookalike, but confirm the wet habitat, narrow leaves, and flexible twigs together.
- Never strip bark from any tree you cannot name.

== HOW TO USE ==
- Scrape the thin green-white inner bark from young twigs or small branches.
- Simmer a small amount, about a teaspoon of shredded bark per cup, in water for 10 to 15 minutes, then strain. It is bitter.
- Keep it weak and occasional. Salicin content swings widely by species and season, so you cannot know the real dose.
- Chewing a fresh twig is the simplest form.
- Never combine it with any other pain or fever medicine.

== CAUTION ==
- This is a comfort measure only. Treat the underlying cause and evacuate for anything serious.

== SEASON ==
- Harvest inner bark in spring, when sap is rising and bark peels easily.
- Take thin strips from a few young twigs. Never cut a ring around a trunk; that kills the tree.
